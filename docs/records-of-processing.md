# Records of processing (accounts)

The record that article 30 of the GDPR asks of a controller, for the one processing of personal data the
site does: the accounts. Without an account, the site keeps nothing about a visitor (see the about page).
Keep it in step with [`src/lib/server/migrations/`](../src/lib/server/migrations) and the privacy page
(`/privacy`).

| Item                     | Accounts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Controller               | The person who runs the site (named in the legal notice, before the site opens to the public).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Purpose                  | Let invited people sign in, keep what they set in their account, and send them the alerts of their zones when they ask for it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Legal basis              | Providing the service the person asks for by creating the account (art. 6(1)(b)).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| People                   | People invited by the person who runs the site.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Data                     | Display name (chosen, need not be real), language, dates of creation and last use; per passkey: identifier, public key, use counter, synced or not, dates; per session: SHA-256 of the token, end date; per invitation: SHA-256 of the code, the inviter's note, dates; per zone: name, shape (a point and a radius, which may be a home; the corners of a drawn zone; a country or an official area), rules (lowest level, kinds of alert), whether the zone was compared once; per device that receives the notifications: the push service's address for it, its encryption keys (RFC 8291), dates of addition and last sending, failures in a row; the device's time zone and the quiet hours choice; per alert that fell in a zone: its title, level, kind, address on the site, the zone, the date, and whether it was sent. |
| Not collected            | E-mail address, password, IP address (not in the database), the visitor's position outside a saved zone.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Recipients               | None: the person who runs the site only. No processor besides the host of the server. A notification is carried, encrypted for the device, by the push service of the browser the person chose (Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft), which cannot read it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Transfers outside the EU | To Canada: the server is rented from OVHcloud in Beauharnois. Canada has an adequacy decision of the European Commission (2002/2/EC), for commercial organisations, which the host is. Notifications: the push service of the person's browser, possibly in the United States; it receives an encrypted message and the device's address only.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Retention                | Account: until deleted by its owner, or 2 years without use. Session: 30 days after last use. Invitation: 30 days after expiry. Passkey challenge: 5 minutes. Device: until removed, unsubscribed, or 5 failures in a row. Alerts of the zones: 30 days, or as long as the alert is in force (removed by the matching, `notify.ts`). Removed every 6 hours by the site (`cleanUp`). Daily dumps of the database: 7 days at most (`deploy/vps/backup.sh` keeps them 5 days, the host's backup 24 hours).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Security                 | Passkeys (no password); secrets stored as SHA-256 only; session cookie HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure on HTTPS; database reachable from the site's container only; account pages never cached.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Rights                   | Export and deletion from the account page; anything else through the contact of the legal notice; complaint to the CNIL.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
