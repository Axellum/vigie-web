# Records of processing (accounts)

The record that article 30 of the GDPR asks of a controller, for the one processing of personal data the
site does: the accounts. Without an account, the site keeps nothing about a visitor (see the about page).
Keep it in step with [`src/lib/server/migrations/`](../src/lib/server/migrations) and the privacy page
(`/privacy`).

| Data                     | Display name (chosen, need not be real), language, units chosen (temperature, wind, heights), dates of creation and last use; per passkey: identifier, public key, use counter, synced or not, dates; per session: SHA-256 of the token, end date; per invitation: SHA-256 of the code, the inviter's note, dates; per recovery code (a set of 8, if the person makes one): SHA-256 of the code, dates of creation and use; per zone: name, shape (a point and a radius, which may be a home; the corners of a drawn zone; a country or an official area), rules (lowest level, kinds of alert), whether the zone was compared once; per device that receives the notifications: for a browser, the push service's address for it and its encryption keys (RFC 8291), for the Android app, its Firebase Cloud Messaging token; dates of addition and last sending, failures in a row; the device's time zone and the quiet hours choice; per place kept for the personal page: name, point (rounded to about 100 m; the home to about 1 km, and the database refuses a finer one), radius, profile, whether it is shown on the public page (never the home), the zone that sends its alerts if there is one; per page: title, blocks (a card, a place of the account, a size), the address of its public page if the person made one; per private link to a page: label, dates of creation, last opening (at most once an hour, no address) and revocation, SHA-256 of the secret (the link is shown once); units chosen; per alert that fell in a zone: its title, level, kind, address on the site, the zone, the date, and whether it was sent. |
| Not collected            | E-mail address, password, IP address (not in the database; the number of tries of an address is counted in the server's memory for an hour or a day and forgotten, for the open sign-up and the recovery codes), the visitor's position outside a saved zone or place.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Recipients               | None: the person who runs the site only. No processor besides the host of the server. A notification is carried, encrypted for the device, by the push service of the browser the person chose (Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft), which cannot read it. In the Android app, it is carried by Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), not encrypted for the device: Google sees its title and body (the alert, the names of the zones).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Transfers outside the EU | To Canada: the server is rented from OVHcloud in Beauharnois. Canada has an adequacy decision of the European Commission (2002/2/EC), for commercial organisations, which the host is. Notifications: the push service of the person's browser, possibly in the United States; it receives an encrypted message and the device's address only. The Android app's: Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), possibly in the United States, which receives the token, the title and the body of the notification.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Retention                | Account: until deleted by its owner, or 2 years without use. Session: 30 days after last use. Invitation: 30 days after expiry. Recovery code: until the person makes a new set, or 30 days after its use. Passkey challenge: 5 minutes. Private link to a page: until its owner revokes it (a revoked link stays in the list, unable to open anything) and no longer than its page or its account. Device: until removed, unsubscribed, or 5 failures in a row. Alerts of the zones: 30 days, or as long as the alert is in force (removed by the matching, `notify.ts`). Removed every 6 hours by the site (`cleanUp`). Daily dumps of the database: 7 days at most (`deploy/vps/backup.sh` keeps them 5 days, the host's backup 24 hours).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Security                 | Passkeys (no password); secrets stored as SHA-256 only; session cookie HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure on HTTPS; database reachable from the site's container only; account pages never cached.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Rights                   | Export and deletion from the account page; anything else through the contact of the legal notice; complaint to the CNIL.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
