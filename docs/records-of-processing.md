# Records of processing (accounts)

The record that article 30 of the GDPR asks of a controller, for the two processings of personal data the
site does: the accounts, and the phones of the Android app that follow places without an account (the bells of
the app, below). Otherwise the site keeps nothing about a visitor (see the about page). Keep it in step with
[`src/lib/server/migrations/`](../src/lib/server/migrations) and the privacy page (`/privacy`).

| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Controller               | The person who runs the site (named in the legal notice of `/about`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Purpose                  | Let people with an account sign in, keep what they set in their account, and send them the alerts of their zones when they ask for it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Legal basis              | Providing the service the person asks for by creating the account (art. 6(1)(b)).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| People                   | People who made an account: invited by the person who runs the site or, when the site is open to sign-ups (`VIGIE_SIGNUP`), on their own.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Data                     | Display name (chosen, need not be real), language, units chosen (temperature, wind, heights), dates of creation and last use; per passkey: identifier, public key, use counter, synced or not, dates; per session: SHA-256 of the token, end date; per invitation: SHA-256 of the code, the inviter's note, dates; per recovery code (a set of 8, if the person makes one): SHA-256 of the code, dates of creation and use; per zone: name, shape (a point and a radius, which may be a home; the corners of a drawn zone; a country or an official area), rules (lowest level, kinds of alert), whether the zone was compared once; per device that receives the notifications: for a browser, the push service's address for it and its encryption keys (RFC 8291), for the Android app, its Firebase Cloud Messaging token and, when the app was linked by a code, SHA-256 of the secret the app was given (the secret stays in the app); dates of addition and last sending, failures in a row; per code that links the app (15 minutes): SHA-256 of the code and of the app's waiting token, the phone's Firebase Cloud Messaging token, the app's secret encrypted with the code until the app picks it up, dates; the device's time zone and the quiet hours choice; per place kept for the personal page: name, point (rounded to about 100 m; the home to about 1 km, and the database refuses a finer one), radius, profile, whether it is shown on the public page (never the home), the zone that sends its alerts if there is one; per page: title, blocks (a card, a place of the account, a size), the address of its public page if the person made one; per private link to a page: label, dates of creation, last opening (at most once an hour, no address) and revocation, SHA-256 of the secret (the link is shown once); units chosen; per alert that fell in a zone: its title, level, kind, address on the site, the zone, the date, and whether it was sent. |

| Recipients               | None: the person who runs the site only. No processor besides the host of the server. A notification is carried, encrypted for the device, by the push service of the browser the person chose (Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft), which cannot read it. In the Android app, it is carried by Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), not encrypted for the device: Google sees its title and body (the alert, the names of the zones).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

Canada has an adequacy decision of the European Commission (2002/2/EC), for commercial organisations, which the host is. Notifications: the push service of the person's browser, possibly in the United States; it receives an encrypted message and the device's address only. The Android app's: Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), possibly in the United States, which receives the token, the title and the body of the notification.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Retention                | Account: until deleted by its owner, or 2 years without use. Session: 30 days after last use. Invitation: 30 days after expiry. Recovery code: until the person makes a new set, or 30 days after its use. Passkey challenge: 5 minutes. Code that links the app: until the app picks up its secret, or 15 minutes. Private link to a page: until its owner revokes it (a revoked link stays in the list, unable to open anything) and no longer than its page or its account. Device: until removed, unsubscribed, or 5 failures in a row. Alerts of the zones: 30 days, or as long as the alert is in force (removed by the matching, `notify.ts`). Removed every 6 hours by the site (`cleanUp`). Daily dumps of the database: 7 days at most (`deploy/vps/backup.sh` keeps them 5 days, the host's backup 24 hours).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Security                 | Passkeys (no password); secrets stored as SHA-256 only; session cookie HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure on HTTPS; database reachable from the site's container only; account pages never cached.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Rights                   | Export and deletion from the account page; anything else through the contact of the legal notice; complaint to the CNIL.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

## The Android app without an account

In the app, the bell of a place asks for its alerts without an account (`src/lib/server/appDevices.ts`,
`/account/app/device`).

| Item                     | The app's bells                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Controller               | The person who runs the site (named in the legal notice of `/about`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Purpose                  | Send the alerts of the places a person chose in the Android app, without an account.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Legal basis              | Providing the service the person asks for by turning a bell on (art. 6(1)(b)).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| People                   | People who turned on the bell of a place in the app.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Data                     | Per phone: its Firebase Cloud Messaging token; SHA-256 of the secret the app was given (the secret stays in the app); language, time zone, quiet hours choice; dates of addition, last sign from the app and last sending, failures in a row. Per place with a bell: name (as the person typed it), point rounded to two decimals (about 1 km), radius, lowest level, whether it was compared once. Per alert that fell in a place: its title, level, kind, address on the site, the place, the date, and whether it was sent. |
| Not collected            | Name, account, e-mail address, IP address (new phones are counted per address in the server's memory for an hour, then forgotten), a point finer than about 1 km.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Recipients               | None: the person who runs the site only. No processor besides the host of the server. The notifications are carried by Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), not encrypted for the phone: Google sees their title and body (the alert, the names of the places).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Transfers outside the EU | To Canada (the server, as for the accounts). Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), possibly in the United States, which receives the token, the title and the body of the notification.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Retention                | Until every bell is turned off in the app (the app asks the server to forget the phone), Google says the token is gone, 5 failures in a row, or 60 days without a sign from the app (removed every 6 hours, `cleanUp`). Alerts of the places: 30 days, or as long as the alert is in force. Daily dumps: 7 days at most.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Security                 | Secret stored as SHA-256 only, sent by the app in a header; answers never cached; at most 10 places and a ceiling of phones (`VIGIE_MAX_APP_DEVICES`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Rights                   | Turning the bells off erases everything at once; anything else through the contact of the legal notice; complaint to the CNIL.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
