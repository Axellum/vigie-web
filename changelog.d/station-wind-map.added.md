- The map can show the wind measured now by the Météo-France stations and the offshore buoys ("Measured wind" among the layers, off until turned on): a badge per station with the mean wind in km/h or knots, a beak toward where the wind goes, the gust in small when it is well above the mean, the strongest kept where badges would touch, and a popup with the station, its altitude and the time of the measure. A small card at the top left gives the time, the unit and the legend; the choice is kept in the browser. Needs a Vigie API with `GET /v1/stations/wind`.
