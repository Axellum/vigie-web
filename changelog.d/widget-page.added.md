- A widget page (`/widget`) for a desktop app: a place, a surf spot or both in three sizes (small, medium, large) and the
  three themes of the common charter (sky, dark, light). The place stays after the `#` and never reaches the server; the
  page shows the official warnings that hold it, the rain of the next hour, the wind measured nearby and, for a spot, the
  verdicts of its activities and its tide (`docs/widget.md`).
