- The widget page (`/widget`, the Windows widgets board): the medium and large widgets are the home page of the Home
  Assistant card itself, drawn smaller to fill the widget; the medium one shows the next 24 hours or the next days, as
  chosen in the app (`fc=days`), the large one both with the facts of now. The small widget is a frise: the weather now,
  the place and a discreet warning, then the next hour as its bars of rain followed by the next hours or days, and the
  sentence of the rain under it, as the card says it. `frame=bare` is for a board asked for no title band. Where the card
  draws no home page of France, the medium and large widgets show the frise, larger (the large one with the days). With
  a place, the surf spot is no longer drawn (the app still gets it in the summary).
