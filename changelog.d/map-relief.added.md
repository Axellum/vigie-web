- The map shows the relief of France: the IGN's hill shading from zoom 6, the finer shading of its LiDAR HD survey from zoom 12, and, when ticked, its contour lines from zoom 12 (the closest the map goes), over mainland France, Corsica and the overseas departments, under the rivers, roads and names, in the light and dark themes. It is on by default and lives in a new "Map" family of the layers rail, where it can be turned off; the choice is kept in the browser. The tiles come straight from the IGN's Géoplateforme (`data.geopf.fr`, Licence Ouverte 2.0), which therefore receives the visitor's IP address, as the about page now says; the site's server is not on the way.
