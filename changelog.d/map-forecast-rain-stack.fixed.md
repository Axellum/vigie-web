- Map, forecast rain: a view wider than the Europe or the France grid shows the coarser model around it, instead of
  rain that stops on a straight line; each place is drawn by one model only, and on a thin band along its edges the
  finer model fades into the coarser one, so that two models that disagree no longer meet on a line. Another lead time,
  or a zoom from Europe to France, no longer blanks the rain while the new images load: the rain shown stays until they
  are ready.
