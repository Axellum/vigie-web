- **Open sign-up and recovery codes.** With `VIGIE_SIGNUP=open` anyone can make an account at `/join` with a name and a passkey; by default the site keeps its invitations. Three limits ask nothing of the visitor: a ceiling on the accounts (`VIGIE_MAX_ACCOUNTS`, 1,000), a ceiling on the accounts made in 24 hours (`VIGIE_SIGNUPS_PER_DAY`, 50) and a small share of tries for each address (kept in memory). Without an e-mail address, a lost passkey would lose the account, so the account page makes a set of 8 recovery codes, shown once and kept as a fingerprint; `/recover` turns one into a 30-minute invitation that adds a passkey (migration 007, with its way back). The sign-in page offers both links; the privacy page, the records of processing and the export list the codes' dates, never the codes.
