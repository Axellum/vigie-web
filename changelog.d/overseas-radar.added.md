- The rain radar card now also shows the overseas radars of Météo-France (the Antilles, La Réunion, New Caledonia) when the map is centred on one of them; the card still shows France's radar over metropolitan France. Cayenne, Mayotte, Papeete and Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon have no radar mosaic, so no card. The site relays `/data/radar.json?area=` and `/data/radar/<area>/<epoch>/<z>/<x>/<y>.png` from the API's `GET /v1/radar/{area}` (the API must be updated first).
