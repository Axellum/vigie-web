- An "Is it normal?" card sets the forecast high of the day against the normals (1991-2020) and records of the month at the nearest Météo-France station, with a gauge from the cold record to the heat record; its detail lists the month at the station (mean high and low, rain, days of frost and heat, sunshine, records and their day). France and its overseas territories only. Needs a Vigie API with `GET /v1/climate/normals`.
