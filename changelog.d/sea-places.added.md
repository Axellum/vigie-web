- Places of the coast (`/sea/places`, `/sea/places/<id>`): the ports, slipways, anchorages, dive sites and bathing sites of the
  French coast, by kind, by name or town, or around your position (rounded to a kilometre, asked of the site only on a click). A
  place says what it is and what is not known (depth, fee, state, permission), the class of the water of a bathing site over
  2021 to 2024 as a word and not as the water of today, the tide where the model has one, the places near it, and what the surf
  spots around it say for the activity you chose, with how far the nearest one is. A catalogue, not a forecast: no place is judged
  and nothing is for safety. The named places are in the sitemap, the others are not offered to search engines (needs a Vigie API
  with `GET /v1/sea/places`).
