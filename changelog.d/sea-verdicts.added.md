- Surf spot page, "Should you go?": a verdict (good, possible or poor) for surf, kite, wing and windsurf, boat and shore
  fishing, hour by hour, with the reason in words, the best slot of the next 24 hours, the tide of each day (its
  coefficient called an estimate) and the coastal warnings, then the nearest CANDHIS wave buoy (its position only: Vigie
  does not read its measurements yet). The verdicts are rules of Vigie, written on the page; they are not for safety
  (needs a Vigie API with `GET /v1/activities/{spot_id}` and `GET /v1/surf/buoys`, on its `main` of 8 November 2026).
