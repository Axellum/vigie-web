- The detail of the weather card shows the chance of rain over the next 24 hours from Météo-France's ensemble forecast (PE-AROME): one bar per window of three hours (the chance of 1 mm or more, with its value and the hour it ends at the place), and chips for rain of 5 mm or more, gusts above 56 km/h, storm, hail and snow from 10 %. It says that the figures are a chance for the area of about 5 km, not for one exact spot. France only, nothing is shown elsewhere; the "Risk" column of the days (Open-Meteo) is unchanged. Needs a Vigie API with `probabilities` in `GET /v1/point`.
