- Offshore buoys (`/sea/buoys`): the latest hourly record of Météo-France's buoys out at sea (the Mediterranean, the Bay of
  Biscay, the Antilles), grouped by sea: waves (height, period, direction), water, wind and gusts, air, pressure and the hour
  of the record, from the API's `GET /v1/sea/buoys`. Linked from the page of the sea and listed in the sitemap.
