- The sea page is a map in a frame: the answer and the list of spots in a panel on the left, the four activities in a bar
  over the map, and the families of layers (the spots, the wind, the rain) in a rail beside it. Each spot has a pin with its
  verdict, the figures of the hour for the activity (the swell for the surf, the wind for the kite and the boat, the tide for
  shore fishing) and the way the tide goes; a pin opens the card of the spot, with four tabs (its verdicts; the surf, with
  where the beach faces and where the swell and the wind come from; the tide; the weather), beside the map on a computer and
  as a sheet at the bottom of the screen on a phone. The figures are the API's; not for safety.
