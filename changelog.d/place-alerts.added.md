- "My places" has an **Alerts** switch for each place, the home included: on, the place gets a circle zone of its own point and radius (the lowest level and the kinds a new zone starts from), so that its official warnings and hazards reach the devices of the account; off, that zone is removed. If the place is renamed, moved or resized, its zone follows, and a zone that moved is compared again without notifying. Deleting a place leaves its zone as it is (the question says so); the level, the kinds, the devices and the quiet hours stay on the account page.
