- The map shows the rain radar of Météo-France over metropolitan France: a small card at the bottom left with the time of the image, a Play button over the last 35 minutes, a slider, the legend (0.1 to 64 mm/h) and the credit. It appears when the map is on France, folds into a button, and says it is measured by radar, not a forecast. The site relays the tiles of the API (`/data/radar/...`, not counted by the rate limit of the site's own files, kept a day by browsers) and asks the list of images once every 5 minutes at most. Needs a Vigie API with `GET /v1/radar/france`.
