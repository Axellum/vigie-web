- Android: the sea map draws the forecast wind as the site's maps do: thin trails that follow Météo-France's wind at 10 m for the hour nearest to now (AROME over France, ARPEGE elsewhere, the site's `/data/wind-field.json` and its images), in the colours of 40 and 60 km/h and more; a card gives the hour of the forecast and the two colours, the layers' menu its legend. Off at first, turned on in the layers' menu, the choice kept on the phone. The trails are drawn about 30 times a second while the map is shown, never in the background; with the phone's animations off they stay still.
