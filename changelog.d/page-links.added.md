- The private links of "My page", server side: a page can be shown on a screen (a television, a tablet on a wall) to whoever holds a secret link, without signing in. The secret is shown once and only its SHA-256 is kept; a link can be revoked; it shows the places of the page, never the home, with the units and language of the account; reads are kept for a minute. An address under `/tv/p/` is sent with no referrer, no cache and no index by the site itself, and the Deck's access log skips it. The screens that use it come in a later change.
